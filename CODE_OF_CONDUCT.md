# Code of conduct

SparePage is built by students and volunteers, and we want everyone who takes part to feel welcome.

**Be kind and be useful.** Be respectful, assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not
the person. Harassment, insults and discrimination are not welcome in issues, pull requests or any
other space for this project.

**What happens if it is not followed.** Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and
block people who keep breaking these rules.

**Reporting.** Email support@sparepage.app. Reports are read by the maintainers and treated
privately.
