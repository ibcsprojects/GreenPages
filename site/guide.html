How to print fewer pages from Google Docs

A checklist you can do by hand. GreenPages looks for steps 2 to 8 for you.

Before you print

  1. Know your page count. In Docs, open Tools, then Word count. It shows the number of pages, so you can tell whether your changes helped.
  2. Remove highlights. Highlighted text uses a lot of ink. Select the text, open the highlight colour in the toolbar and choose None. Bold or underline marks the same words without the ink.
  3. Bring oversized text back to size. Body text at 16pt or 18pt fills pages fast. Select it and set 11pt or 12pt. Keep headings larger.
  4. Check the margins. Open File, then Page setup. The default is one inch (2.54 cm) on every side. Much wider margins waste paper. Going below half an inch can cut off text on some printers.
  5. Remove stacked blank lines and blank pages. Turn on View, then Show non-printing characters. Every extra paragraph mark that only holds a blank line is visible, and you can delete it. A blank last page is usually one stray paragraph.
  6. Replace hard page breaks. A page break that leaves most of a page empty forces a new sheet. Delete it, or use Insert, then Horizontal line, instead.
  7. Shrink large images. A picture that fills a page makes a page of its own. Click it and drag a corner handle until it fits with the text.
  8. Tighten wordy paragraphs. Read each long paragraph and cut phrases like “in order to” (use “to”) and “due to the fact that” (use “because”). Fewer words means fewer lines.

In the print dialog

Press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on a Mac). In Chrome's print window:

  1. Print both sides. Under More settings, choose Two-sided. It halves the sheets you use. If your printer cannot, it may let you print the odd pages and then the even pages.
  2. Print only the pages you need. Use Pages, then Custom, and type a range such as 2-4.
  3. Fit more on a sheet. Under More settings, set Pages per sheet to 2 for drafts and notes you only need to read.
  4. Use black and white. Set Color to Black and white unless colour matters. It saves colour ink.
  5. Check the preview. Look at the page count and the last page before you click Print.
  6. Pick the right printer and paper size. Sending a job to the wrong place wastes the whole print.

Let GreenPages do it

Steps 2 to 8 above are what GreenPages looks for. It lists each problem, shows the saving, and applies the fix when you click. See how to install it. If your document is not in Google Docs, save it as a PDF and try the PDF check.
