How to print on both sides in Google Docs

Printing on both sides uses half the sheets, and it is the biggest single saving for most documents. In Google Docs it is one option in the print window, if your printer supports it.

If your printer does it for you

  1. Press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on a Mac), or choose File, then Print.
  2. Pick your printer, then click More settings or More options.
  3. Turn on Two-sided (the wording varies). Choose Flip on long edge for a normal portrait document, or Flip on short edge for a landscape one that opens like a notepad.
  4. Check the page count in the preview, then print.

If the option is missing

Some printers only offer double-sided in their own settings. In Chrome you can open your computer’s print window instead: choose Print using system dialog (Ctrl+Shift+P, or Cmd+Option+P on a Mac) and look for a Two-sided or Duplex setting.

By hand

If your printer cannot do it at all, print the odd pages first (type 1,3,5,7 and so on in the Pages box), put the printed stack back in the tray the right way up, then print the even pages. Test with a two-page document first to learn which way your printer wants the paper.

Or let SparePage do it. Before you print, press Ctrl+P or Cmd+P and it shows the page count and a reminder to print double-sided. See how to install it.
