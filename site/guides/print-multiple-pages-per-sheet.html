How to print more than one page per sheet

Printing two pages on each side of a sheet, sometimes called 2-up, cuts paper use in half again. Four to a sheet is smaller still. Most print windows have a Pages per sheet setting.

Set it up

  1. Press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on a Mac) to open the print window.
  2. Open More settings and look for Pages per sheet. Choose 2 or 4.
  3. If you cannot see it, open your computer’s own print window (in Chrome, Print using system dialog). On a Mac, look under Layout. On Windows, look in the printer’s Preferences.
  4. Look at the preview before you print.

When it is a good idea

Two to a sheet is fine for body text at normal size. Four to a sheet shrinks text a lot, so it suits slides, drafts and notes more than anything you need to read closely. Combine it with printing on both sides and four pages cost a single sheet.

Or let SparePage do it. It shows the page count before you print, so you can tell what the layout will cost. See how to install it.
