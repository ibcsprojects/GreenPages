How to save ink when printing documents

Most of the ink in an everyday document goes on colour fills, big images and dark backgrounds, not on the text. A few settings and edits cut it a lot.

In the print window

  1. Press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on a Mac).
  2. Set Color to Black and white unless the colour matters.
  3. Open More settings and leave Background graphics off, so page and text backgrounds are not printed.
  4. Check your printer’s own settings for a Draft, Economy or Toner save mode. It is fine for anything you only need to read.

In the document

  • Replace highlighting with an underline or bold. See how to remove highlighting.
  • Make large pictures smaller. A page-sized image is one of the heaviest things you can print.
  • Avoid dark page colours and large coloured blocks.

Before you click Print

Look at the preview. A last page with one line on it, or a blank page, costs a full sheet and its ink.

Or let SparePage do it. It lists highlights and page-sized images, with the fix and the saving for each. See how to install it.
