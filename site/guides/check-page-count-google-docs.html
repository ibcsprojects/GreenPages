How to see the page count of a Google Doc before you print

You can see a Google Doc’s page count in two places: the Word count window and the print window. Check it before you print, because a stray last page costs a whole sheet.

Word count

Open Tools, then Word count (Ctrl+Shift+C, or Cmd+Shift+C on a Mac). The window lists pages, words, characters and characters without spaces. Tick Display word count while typing to keep a small counter in the corner.

The print window

Press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on a Mac). The preview shows each page and the total, and the window usually tells you how many sheets of paper the job will use, which depends on your two-sided setting.

Why the numbers can differ

Page count depends on paper size, margins and fonts. A document set to Letter paper and sent to an A4 printer will not break in the same places. Check File, then Page setup, and pick the paper size your printer uses.

Or let SparePage do it. It shows the exact page count in the panel, and again when you press Ctrl+P or Cmd+P, along with anything left to fix. See how to install it.
