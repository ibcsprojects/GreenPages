Privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026. Applies to the SparePage Chrome extension and this website.

SparePage does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. The extension runs entirely in your browser. We do not operate any server that receives your documents or information about you.

What the extension accesses

DataWhyWhere it goes
The content and formatting of the Google Doc you have open To find print waste and apply the fixes you accept Read from and written back to Google's Docs API, directly from your browser. Never sent to us.
The PDF version of that document, from Google Docs' own export To count its exact printed pages Downloaded from Google (docs.google.com, which hands the file to a googleusercontent.com address) into your browser's memory, counted, and discarded.
A Google sign-in token So the Docs API knows you allowed access Managed by Chrome. SparePage never sees your password.
Your SparePage settings To remember your preferences Stored on your device with Chrome's storage. Not synced to us.

Google permission

SparePage asks for one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents. It uses it only to read the document you are working in when you open SparePage, and to edit that document when you click Apply. It does not browse your Drive, other files, email or contacts.

SparePage' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is not used for advertising, is not transferred to anyone, and is not read by people.

No AI services, no analytics

Suggestions, including shorter wording for long paragraphs, are made by rules that run in your browser. No document text is sent to any AI service. The extension contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

The PDF check and Word file pages

The text box on the home page works the same way: what you type or paste is checked in your browser and is never sent to us.

The PDF check and the Word file tool on this website read the file you choose inside your browser. The file is not uploaded anywhere. The Word file tool builds a corrected copy in your browser's memory and hands it to you as a download; we never receive it.

The Word add-in

The Word add-in reads the document you have open and edits it only when you click Apply. It asks Word for permission to read and write the document. Checking happens on your computer; the document is not sent to us or anyone else. Microsoft requires add-ins to load their Office.js library from Microsoft's servers, so Microsoft may see that the add-in opened; see Microsoft's privacy statement.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to operate the service; see GitHub's privacy statement. We add no cookies or trackers.

Removing access

You can remove SparePage' access at any time from your Google Account connections page, and uninstall the extension from chrome://extensions. Uninstalling deletes its settings from your device.

Children

SparePage collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Schools and parents can review exactly what it does in the public source code.

Who runs SparePage

SparePage was created by Manav Purswani and Cata Gamero and is maintained as an open-source project by the contributors to its public repository. We hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Google keeps your documents under its own terms and privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and the history is visible in the project's public repository. Questions about privacy: email privacy@sparepage.app, or open an issue on GitHub.
