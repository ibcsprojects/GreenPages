Privacy policy

Effective 6 November 2026. Applies to the GreenPages Chrome extension and this website.

GreenPages does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. The extension runs entirely in your browser. We do not operate any server that receives your documents or information about you.

What the extension accesses

DataWhyWhere it goes
The content and formatting of the Google Doc you have open To find print waste and apply the fixes you accept Read from and written back to Google's Docs API, directly from your browser. Never sent to us.
The PDF version of that document, from Google Docs' own export To count its exact printed pages Downloaded from Google (docs.google.com, which hands the file to a googleusercontent.com address) into your browser's memory, counted, and discarded.
A Google sign-in token So the Docs API knows you allowed access Managed by Chrome. GreenPages never sees your password.
Your GreenPages settings To remember your preferences Stored on your device with Chrome's storage. Not synced to us.

Google permission

GreenPages asks for one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents. It uses it only to read the document you are working in when you open GreenPages, and to edit that document when you click Apply. It does not browse your Drive, other files, email or contacts.

GreenPages' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is not used for advertising, is not transferred to anyone, and is not read by people.

No AI services, no analytics

Suggestions, including shorter wording for long paragraphs, are made by rules that run in your browser. No document text is sent to any AI service. The extension contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

The PDF check page

The PDF check on this website reads the file you choose inside your browser. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to operate the service; see GitHub's privacy statement. We add no cookies or trackers.

Removing access

You can remove GreenPages' access at any time from your Google Account connections page, and uninstall the extension from chrome://extensions. Uninstalling deletes its settings from your device.

Children

GreenPages collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Schools and parents can review exactly what it does in the public source code.

Who runs GreenPages

GreenPages is an open-source project maintained by the contributors to its public repository. We hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Google keeps your documents under its own terms and privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and the history is visible in the project's public repository. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
